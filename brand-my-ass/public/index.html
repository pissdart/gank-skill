Questions

Yes, really.

Is this real?

Yes. Real skin, real needle, real ink. Every winning placement is tattooed on me and documented on video. I'm doing this because I'm building a game and I need runway more than I need an unbranded ass.

Where does the money go?

Into finishing my game: dev time, art, sound, a launch budget. The funding bar at the top shows the running total of all leading bids against the dev budget.

What can I tattoo?

Your logo, wordmark, or a short line of text, in black ink, sized to the placement. No hate, no slurs, no explicit imagery, nothing illegal, and I keep final veto. Artwork is agreed with the leading bidder before anything is inked.

Why is nothing on the front, spine or sides?

Every placement is on my back side and hidden from the front. The spine itself, the flanks and the hips are off the table: that skin is reserved for my own work. The back is laid out as a grid, two columns and four rows either side of the spine, so the placements sit in clean pairs. What you're bidding on is exactly what's drawn on the model, nothing else.

Do I pay now?

Only a $250 deposit, charged by card through Stripe when you place a bid. It's refunded automatically the moment someone outbids you. If you're leading when the clock stops, the deposit counts toward your total and I invoice the balance; payment clears before the needle does. If you win and don't settle within 14 days, I keep the deposit and the placement passes to the next bidder.

Can I take a bigger piece?

Yes. Neighbouring placements can be treated as one canvas: both cheeks, a whole row across the back, or a full column down one side. Lead more than one placement at close and a bundle discount comes off the combined total: 2 placements → 10% off. It's applied on the invoice, not on the bids, so every auction stays a straight race.

How big are the placements?

Sizes are listed on each row and drawn to scale on the model. The cheeks are the biggest canvases at roughly 15 × 15 cm; each cell of the back grid is about 9 × 9 cm, and two or more neighbouring cells can be combined into one piece with the bundle discount.

What do I get besides my logo on your ass?

Photos and video of the session, before and after shots you can use however you like, a permanent mention in the game's credits, and the knowledge that your brand is, quite literally, on me.
