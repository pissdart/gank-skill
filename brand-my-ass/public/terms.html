Auction terms.

Version 2026-09-05. All amounts are in United States dollars.

What this is

Each placement is a separate, public auction for the right to have your artwork tattooed on the operator's body at that location. Every placement opens at the stated opening bid and each new bid raises the current price by a fixed step. By submitting a bid you confirm the company information is accurate and that the bid represents genuine sponsorship interest.

Public information

Your company name, website, bid amount and any customization text appear publicly in the relevant placement history and on the 3D model. Your email address is kept private and used only to contact you about the auction.

Leading bidders

When the auction ends, the operator will contact each leading bidder directly to confirm the sponsorship, agree the artwork, schedule and commercial details, and invoice the winning amount. No payment or card information is collected through this website. Nothing is tattooed until payment has cleared. If a leading bidder does not respond or does not pay within 14 days of being contacted, the placement passes to the next highest bidder in the history.

Artwork

Artwork is black ink, sized to the placement, and must be a logo, wordmark or short line of text. The operator will refuse hateful, discriminatory, sexually explicit, unlawful, infringing or deceptive artwork, and keeps final say over what goes on their body. If artwork cannot be agreed in good faith, the placement passes to the next bidder.

Deliverables

The winning sponsor receives photos and video of the tattoo session and the finished placement, a licence to use them in their own marketing, and a credit in the game the auction funds. The tattoo is permanent; the operator does not commit to any particular level of future public exposure of it.

Removal of bids

The operator may remove bids that are unlawful, abusive, obviously fraudulent, or made with invalid contact details. Removed bids disappear from the public history and the price reverts to the previous valid bid.

Contact

Questions about the auction go to the operator via the link in the footer of the auction page.
